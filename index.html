BRIEF BIOGRAPHY

My name is Eberechukwu Prosper Chibuike, and I am a student of MIVA Open University pursuing a degree in computing, technology professional, system analyst, web developer, and computer networking specialist based in Aba, Abia State, Nigeria. He is passionate about technology, innovation, problem-solving, and the development of practical digital solutions that help individuals, businesses, and organizations improve their operations and achieve their objectives.

My interest in technology developed from a desire to understand how computers work, how technical problems can be solved, and how technology can be used to make everyday activities easier and more efficient. What began as an interest in computers grew into a professional path that introduced me to computer hardware, software installation, troubleshooting, networking, website development, and information systems.

Why I Decided to Pursue a University Degree at MIVA Open University

Although practical experience has played an important role in my professional development, I recognise that professional growth requires continuous learning and a strong educational foundation.

My decision to pursue a university degree at Miva Open University reflects my desire to broaden my knowledge, strengthen my intellectual foundation, and prepare myself for greater professional responsibilities.

I understand that practical skills and formal education offer different but complementary benefits. Practical experience teaches a person how to approach real-world challenges, while formal education provides opportunities to study concepts systematically, develop critical thinking, understand theoretical foundations, and gain a broader perspective on a chosen field.

By pursuing a degree, I am taking deliberate steps to combine the knowledge I have acquired through professional practice with the academic training that higher education provides. My decision is not simply about obtaining a certificate. It is about personal development, intellectual growth, expanding my opportunities, and becoming better equipped to contribute meaningfully to my profession. I believe that education should not be viewed as something reserved for a particular age or stage of life. Rather, it is a continuing process that allows individuals to improve themselves, discover new possibilities, and prepare for future opportunities. Enrolling at Miva Open University represents an important part of this journey.






Combining My Studies with My Profession

One of the defining aspects of my current journey is my decision to combine university education with my professional responsibilities. Balancing work and academic studies requires discipline, effective time management, commitment, and the willingness to make sacrifices. Both responsibilities demand attention, and managing them together requires me to organise my time carefully and remain focused on my priorities.

As I continue my studies at Miva Open University, I also remain engaged in my professional interests in system analysis, website development, computer networking, and IT solutions.

My professional experience gives me practical situations through which I can appreciate the concepts and principles I encounter in my studies. Similarly, my academic journey provides opportunities to deepen my understanding, question existing assumptions, and approach professional challenges from new perspectives. This combination allows me to pursue both practical competence and academic development.

Why Web Technologies?

The web is the most open platform ever created. Anyone with a browser can use what you build, from anywhere in the world. That reach is what excites me: a single well-made website can educate, inform, sell, connect people, or solve a problem for thousands of users at the same time.

Build things, break things, fix things – that is how learning sticks.
